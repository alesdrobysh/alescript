Language Primitives

Core Concepts

Everything in alescript maps to the art of brewing. Think like a brewmaster.

🌾

Brews & Ingredients

Variables are brewing ingredients. Water, barley, hops, and yeast each contribute to growth rate.

brew lager from water, 1 barley, 2 hops, 1 yeast.
⏳

Fermentation

Values don't compute instantly — they ferment over time, forcing strategic thinking.

wait for 5 days.
taste lager. // "12.5% ABV"
🎯

Aging

Need exact values? Let your brew mature to a precise ABV.

age lager until 5.2%.
🍻

Beer Arithmetic

Blend, fortify, and filter — math expressed through brewing metaphors.

blend ipa with stout.
fortify porter by 3.
filter ipa by 2.
📜

Recipes

Functions are recipes. The last line is the return value.

recipe my_pilsner()
  brew pilsner from water, barley.
  age pilsner until 4.8%.
  pilsner
end.
🧪

Brewmaster's Judgment

Conditionals use the brewmaster's intuition — comparisons have ±10% imprecision!

judge if lager is stronger than 5.0%:
  toast "a strong lager!"
end.

03

Fibonacci Sequence

fibonacci.ales 
recipe fibonacci(n)
    brew a from water, barley.
    wait for 1 day.
    brew b from water, barley.
    brew temp from water.

    if n is 0:
        b
    else:
        repeat n times:
            relabel temp as a.
            blend a with b.
            relabel b as a.
            relabel a as temp.
        b
    end.
end.

toast fibonacci(10). // "55% ABV"
